Civic Route Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Civic Route helps you find the government office to contact about an issue. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your location is used only to look up your officials.

What the app uses

  • Your location. Either your device's location (only after you tap "Use my location" and allow the permission) or a street address you type in. It is used to work out your county, state and congressional district.
  • Choices you make in the app, such as saved topics, starred officials and progress notes. These are stored only on your device.

Where your information goes

  • U.S. Census Bureau geocoder receives your coordinates (or the address you typed) to return your county and congressional district.
  • Civic Route's lookup relay (a small service run on Cloudflare) receives your approximate coordinates and forwards a request to Open States to find your state legislators. It also forwards requests to Congress.gov for your federal officials; those requests contain only your state and district, not your location. The relay does not store requests, and we do not keep logs of them. Cloudflare, as our infrastructure provider, may process network data to operate the service.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you across apps or sites.

What stays on your device

Your coordinates, your last resolved county and district, saved topics, progress notes, and the last list of officials shown (so the app works offline). You can remove all of it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app has no accounts. The app does not currently include analytics or crash-reporting services. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Contact information shown in the app

Phone numbers and websites come from public government sources and may be out of date. Please confirm with the office before relying on them.

Children

Civic Route is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

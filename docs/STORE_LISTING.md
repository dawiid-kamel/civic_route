## Full description
Not sure who handles a pothole, a landlord problem, or a Medicare question? Civic Route tells you which level of government is responsible and gives you the right office to contact, with a phone number, website, and what to say.

- Pick a topic like housing, transportation, healthcare, or taxes
- See who handles it: federal, state, or county
- Find your U.S. House member, senators, and state legislators from your location or an address you type
- Call or visit a website in one tap, and copy a ready-made call script or email
- Save topics and track your progress
- Works offline with your last results
- No account, no ads, no tracking

Local county offices are currently built in for Baltimore County, Maryland. Federal and state officials work anywhere in the U.S. Contact information can change, so confirm with the office before relying on it.
